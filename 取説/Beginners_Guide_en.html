Connection Checker Beginner's Guide

An app that checks, with one click, whether the connection you use for Claude goes through a VPN. It takes about 3 minutes to read.

What this app does

Shows if you're safe, in colorGreen, yellow or red tells you whether your connection looks fine.
Shows what's flaggedIt checks Claude's six addresses one by one and shows them in a table.
Tells you how to fix itIf something is wrong, the next steps appear on the screen.
Keeps a historyEach result is saved so you can look back later.

Before you start

  • A Windows 11 PC
    This app is for Windows 11.
  • The downloaded zip file
    It is named "KaisenChecker_v1.3.0.zip".
  • About 3 minutes
    No installation needed.
  • The name of your VPN software
    Only if you use a VPN. You'll use it in STEP 6.

A VPN (= a service that sends your traffic through another place so your location is harder to tell) can make Claude see your connection as "not a normal home connection". This app checks for that.

How to use

STEP 1

Extract the zip file

Right-click the downloaded zip (= a box that holds many files as one; you can't use what's inside until you open it) file. Choose Extract All, then click Extract.

STEP 2

Double-click "回線チェッカー.exe"

Open the extracted folder and double-click 回線チェッカー.exe. An exe (= a file that runs as an app when you double-click it) file.

Careful If you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info and then Run anyway. This is SmartScreen (= Windows asking "are you sure about this app?").
Tip The _internal folder next to it is needed to run the app. Don't delete it or move it somewhere else.
STEP 3

Look at the colored bar at the top

When the window opens, the app starts checking by itself. After a few seconds a colored bar appears at the top. Its color and words are the result.

Green · Safe Unlikely to get you banned.
Yellow · Caution Some connections need attention.
Red · Danger Likely to get you banned.

BAN (= your account gets blocked and you can no longer use it).

▶See the yellow and red screens (examples)

These screens were made with example data. Real results may show different domains or countries.

STEP 4

Look at the six addresses in the table

The table under the bar lists Claude's six domains (= names of places on the internet, like claude.ai). If the text in the "Connection" column is green, that one is fine.

The last row, "other sites", is only for comparison. Even if it goes through a VPN, it doesn't change the result.

STEP 5

Read "Why" and "What to do"

The white box under the table explains why you got this result and what to do. If it says Safe, you can just close the app.

If it's yellow or red, follow "What to do" in order. STEP 6 onward is for when some domains go through a VPN.

STEP 6 · when a VPN is used

Click "Copy domains to exclude"

This button can be clicked only when some domains go through a VPN. Click it to copy those domain names.

You'll paste what you copied in STEP 7.

Tip If the button text looks faded, no domains go through a VPN. You can skip this step.
STEP 7 · when a VPN is used

Paste them into your VPN's exclusions

Open your VPN software and find its "exclusions" or "split tunneling" settings. Paste the copied domain names there (Ctrl+V).

Split tunneling (= a setting that says "keep these sites out of the VPN").

▶Where to find it in each VPN

AdGuard VPN: Exclusions → Add manually, then add each domain.

Surfshark: VPN settings → Bypasser, then add the websites.

Windscribe: Preferences → Split Tunneling, then add the hostnames to exclusions.

Cloudflare WARP: Preferences → Advanced → Split Tunnels, then exclude the domains.

NordVPN, ExpressVPN, Proton VPN: these exclude by app, not by domain. Exclude the Claude app, your browser and Connection Checker.

Menu names can change between versions of the VPN software.

Don't If there are spaces around the name, or it's typed in full-width characters, the VPN may say the domain is invalid. Clear the field and paste again in half-width characters.
STEP 8

Click "Check again"

After changing the settings, go back to Connection Checker and click the blue button on the far left, "Check again". It checks once more.

You did it! When the bar turns green and says Safe, you're done.

Extras: other buttons

▶Switching the language

Click the button in the top-right corner of the window ("日本語" or "English") to switch the language. Click it again to switch back. The language you choose is used next time too.

▶Looking at the history

Click "History folder" to open the folder that holds your history as a CSV (= a simple file that holds a table of data) file. Open history.csv in Excel to see the time and the result of each check.

Your IP address and location are not saved in the history.

▶The app also checks Claude's actual traffic

The last two rows of the table show what the app found by looking at the actual connections of the Claude app (including Claude Code) running on your PC, and of your browser to Claude.

It checks whether the traffic goes through a VPN by looking at which network the traffic leaves from. This shows the real picture even when you exclude apps from the VPN.

If it says "Could not tell (may go through a local proxy)", an ad blocker or security software is relaying the traffic and the app could not see it. In that case, rely on the other rows.

It only reads the list of connections on this PC. The other side's addresses are never shown or saved.

▶Handy features (keyboard, startup check)

Press F5 to check again and Esc to close. Ctrl++ and Ctrl+− change the text size (Ctrl+0 resets it).

Turn on "Check automatically when Windows starts" at the bottom of the window and the app checks every time your PC starts. It shows nothing when everything is safe, and opens its window only for Caution or Danger.

Turn on "Check for a new version" and the app looks on GitHub for a newer version and tells you if there is one.

▶Reading the full manual

Click "Manual" at the bottom of the app to open the detailed manual.

If something goes wrong

QA warning stopped me from opening the app
CauseWindows and antivirus software sometimes stop apps that aren't code-signed, just to be safe.
FixFollow STEP 2: "More info" → "Run anyway". If your antivirus removed the file, restore it by following "If the app won't start" in the manual.
QEverything says "Could not check"
CauseYou may be offline, or an ad blocker may be blocking the check.
FixWait a moment and click "Check again". If it keeps failing, turn off your ad blocker for a while and try again.
QIt says "Danger" even though Claude works fine
CauseWith a VPN that excludes by app (such as NordVPN), the checker itself goes through the VPN.
FixAdd Connection Checker (回線チェッカー.exe) to the VPN's exclusions too.
QI use a VPN browser extension. Is the result right?
CauseThis app can't see VPNs that run as a browser extension.
FixTurn the extension VPN off when you use Claude in the browser.
QIt says the history could not be saved
CauseThe history file is open in Excel or another app.
FixClose Excel, then click "Check again".

Questions

QIf it says Safe, can I be sure I won't be banned?

No. This app only checks whether anything looks suspicious. It doesn't guarantee you won't be banned. Anthropic doesn't publish its criteria.

QWhat information is sent out?

The app connects to Claude's addresses, the supported-countries page, and an IP lookup service. The lookup service receives your IP address. The IP address is never shown or saved. No usage data is sent to the author.

QCan I use it on a work PC?

Your company manages its VPN and network settings. Don't change them yourself; ask the person who manages them. Just checking doesn't change any settings.

QHow do I remove the app?

Move the extracted folder to the Recycle Bin. Your history and settings are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\KaisenChecker, so delete that folder too.
